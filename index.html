Behind the bend

It follows your lid.

The Mac companion reads the MacBook’s real lid sensor and shapes the desktop as you close it. This page is an interactive preview—drag the screen or use the slider to try the effect.

Local build · Apple silicon

Bring the bend to your desktop.

The native companion uses the MacBook’s lid sensor when the model exposes it; sensor availability varies. It needs macOS 14 or later and Screen Recording permission. Screen capture stays on while Foldly is enabled, including with the lid open. Pause or disable Foldly to stop capture. On unsupported hardware, you can still use the manual preview.
